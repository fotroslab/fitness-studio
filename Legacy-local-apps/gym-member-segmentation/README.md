# 🏋️ Gym Member Segmentation & Clustering

An end-to-end, **CRISP-DM-structured unsupervised-learning** project that turns
raw gym-session data into **actionable fitness personas** — and proves the
segmentation is real, not just a pretty scatter plot.

## 📌 Business problem

Gyms run one-size-fits-all programming: same classes, same emails, same
retention offers for everyone. This project segments members into **fitness
personas** (body composition + training intensity + behaviour) so the business
can design per-persona programs, target marketing where it converts, and build
habits with the members most likely to lapse.

## 📊 Datasets (switchable in the sidebar)

| Dataset | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Real — Kaggle** | 973 | [Gym Members Exercise Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/valakhorasani/gym-members-exercise-dataset): demographics, heart-rate metrics, session behaviour, body composition. Honest, continuous data — natural clusters are weak, so feature engineering genuinely matters. |
| **Synthetic — 10 known personas** | 973 | Generated by `generate_data.py`: *HIIT Warrior, Power Builder, Cardio Runner, Mindful Mover, Casual Beginner, Weekend Warrior, Fat-Loss Focused, Plateau Veteran, MetCon Addict, Senior Wellness*. Heart-rate physiology is modelled (max HR ≈ 208 − 0.7 × age). Ground truth is kept so the pipeline can be **validated with the Adjusted Rand Index** — k=10 recovers the true personas with ARI ≈ 0.74. |

## 🧬 Physiological feature engineering

Raw BPM columns are age-confounded (max heart rate falls with age), so the app
engineers:

| Feature | Formula | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `HRR_pct` | (Avg_BPM − Resting_BPM) / (Max_BPM − Resting_BPM) × 100 | **% heart-rate reserve** — the standard sports-science measure of *relative* intensity, comparable across ages |
| `Kcal_per_min` | Calories_Burned / (Session_Duration × 60) | Calorie burn **rate** — how densely a member burns energy, independent of session length |
| `Weekly_Volume_hrs` | Session_Duration × Workout_Frequency | Total weekly **training load** |

Clustering on these engineered features recovers the synthetic personas far
better than raw columns (ARI 0.74 vs 0.59).

## 🧭 The pipeline (6 CRISP-DM tabs)

1. **Business Understanding** — objective, persona strategy, success criteria.
2. **Data Understanding** — distributions (raw + engineered), correlation
   structure (multicollinearity exposed), categorical breakdowns, interactive
   scatter exploration.
3. **Data Preparation** — multicollinearity-aware **feature selection**
   (drops near-duplicates like `Weight`≈`BMI`, `Session`≈`Calories`),
   skewness-driven **log transforms**, optional **IQR outlier clipping**, choice
   of **Standard / Robust / MinMax scaling**, PCA inspection of the scaled space.
4. **Modeling** — **nine algorithms in a head-to-head bake-off**: K-Means,
   MiniBatch K-Means, Agglomerative (Ward), Agglomerative (complete),
   Gaussian Mixture, Spectral Clustering, BIRCH, DBSCAN and HDBSCAN — ranked
   on Silhouette ↑, Davies–Bouldin ↓, Calinski–Harabasz ↑ and (when ground
   truth exists) **ARI vs truth**; per-metric winners highlighted, an
   **overall champion** by combined rank, bootstrap **stability scores**
   (ARI refit on 80% subsamples), elbow + silhouette curves for `k`, PCA
   cluster maps and per-sample silhouette plots.
   - Synthetic data **defaults to k = 10** (its true persona count).
   - Real data **defaults to k = 5** — silhouette mathematically prefers k = 2
     on continuous data, but two segments are not actionable; the app shows
     both sides of the trade-off and lets you choose.
5. **Evaluation** — cluster profile table, **radar chart** of persona shapes,
   box plots per feature, **auto-named personas** (14 archetypes, e.g. *HIIT
   Warrior, Weekend Warrior, Fat-Loss Focused, Plateau Veteran, MetCon Addict,
   Senior Wellness…*) with recommended business actions, and on synthetic data
   an **ARI-vs-k validation curve** + ground-truth crosstab.
6. **Deployment** — live scoring: sliders for a new member's metrics →
   predicted persona, distance-to-centroid confidence chart, downloadable
   segmented CSV.

## 🔑 Key findings

- **Multicollinearity is the silent killer of clustering.** `Weight ↔ BMI`
  (r = 0.85), `Session_Duration ↔ Calories_Burned` (r = 0.91) and
  `Workout_Frequency ↔ Experience_Level` (r = 0.84) double-count single
  signals in Euclidean distance. A de-duplicated feature set segments better
  than all 13 raw features.
- **Relative intensity beats raw BPM.** `HRR_pct` (% heart-rate reserve)
  removed the age confound and lifted persona recovery from ARI 0.59 to 0.74.
- **Silhouette alone misleads.** On the 10-persona synthetic data, silhouette
  peaks near k = 2–3, but the true structure (10 personas) sits at k = 10 with
  the best ARI. Compactness ≠ meaning — cross-check with domain
  interpretability (and ground truth when available).
- **Real behavioural data is continuous.** On the Kaggle data the best
  silhouette is ≈ 0.28 — segments are gradients, not islands. The honest
  response is better features, multiple metrics, and business-driven `k`,
  not cherry-picked `k`.
